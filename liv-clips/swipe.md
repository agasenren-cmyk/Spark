## What the winners share

1. **The room is the star.** The biggest multiple by far is one wide shot of a packed main room
   with the venue's signature feature, named in the text. That is cut type C, and it should open on
   the single widest, brightest frame in the footage.
2. **POV + venue + artist + time.** "POV: it's 3AM inside <venue> with <artist>" is the cleanest
   repeatable format here (38x). Crowd shot from the floor, hands and phones up.
3. **A relatable feeling beats a description.** Lines about the viewer ("you can fake a smile...",
   "where I really am when I tell my parents...", "Hey! You there!") beat lines about the event.
4. **Booth POV with a person moving.** The DJ's biggest reel is behind the decks with someone
   dancing in frame, no text at all.

## What flopped

- Talking-head promoter explainers ("How to register for clubs in Las Vegas" 7.5K, the 3-5x
  smaller edson reels). Information is not what people come to this niche for.
- Generic aesthetic quotes that are not about the footage ("the art of letting it go", 6.7K).
- Dark shots with no clear subject (letsshowcase 7-9K range).

## Applied to the LIV campaign

The campaign pays up to 50K views a clip; every outlier above cleared that many times over, so the
target is formats, not luck.

| Cut | Open on | On-screen text (bank line first, then a new one in the winning style) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C venue montage | widest packed-room shot | "Clubs to visit at least once in your life" / "POV: you walk into LIV Las Vegas and see this" |
| A booth POV | behind the decks, crowd hands up, drop in 1-2 s | "POV: you're behind the decks at LIV Las Vegas" / "the view from the DJ booth at LIV Las Vegas" |
| B story hook | the most unusual moment | "POV: my IG story at 3AM when I said I was going to bed" / "where I really am when I say I'm staying in" |
| any | crowd from the floor | "POV: one night in Vegas" / "POV: inside LIV Las Vegas with <artist>" (only the artist from that night's folder) |

Rules still win over the swipe file: no other venue's name (no OMNIA, no Tao), no facts that
cannot be seen in the footage, no booking CTA. The new lines are outside the brief's text bank;
check the ClipHaus brief allows custom text before posting them.
